Crew Activity Dashboard Privacy Policy

Operated independently by Christopher DeBouck · Effective November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Christopher DeBouck (“I,” “me,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information in connection with the Crew Activity Dashboard mobile application for iOS and Android, and the related hosted service (together, the “App”).

Christopher DeBouck is the independent developer and operator of the App, and the controller of the App service. Crew Activity Dashboard is a workplace operations app that field crews and companies can use in their own workspace. It is not directed to the general public and is not directed to children.

Employers or workspace administrators who use the App control the operational work records created inside their workspace, such as inventory, reports, photos, expenses, and call-ins. I operate the App service and the account infrastructure that makes those workspaces available.

2. What the App does

The App helps crews and supervisors coordinate work on jobsites. Depending on a person’s role and permissions, it may be used to read and post announcements; manage inventory, pick lists, and tools (including QR codes); log truck unloading and truck trips; submit daily reports and site photos; submit expense reports; use project management tools (including equipment rentals and manpower); upload spreadsheets for AI-assisted operational reports; prepare manpower and equipment quotes; and submit or review call-ins for absence or lateness.

3. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information, as verified in the App’s operation. We do not collect precise or approximate device GPS location, microphone or audio recordings, contacts, or calendar data. We do not use advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs.

Account and profile information. When you sign in, our authentication provider (Base44) processes your account credentials (such as email address and password, or another sign-in method offered on the hosted login screen). We store your email address, first name, last name, display name, and phone number (phone number is required when you complete your profile). We also store your role, department access, assigned jobsite, and account approval status.

Operational and workplace records. The App stores the work records you and your colleagues create, such as announcements, inventory and pick-list data, tool records and invoices, truck and unloading records, daily reports (including work progress, manpower entries, delays, and comments), site photo albums, expense reports and receipt details (including amounts and categories), project and quote records, and call-in records (including the date, whether you are absent or late, a reason category you select — which may include options such as Sick, Family Emergency, Transportation, Appointment, Personal, or Other — and a written explanation). These records may include the names or email addresses of the people who created or updated them. Operational work records in a workspace are controlled by that workspace’s administrator.

Photos, files, and other user content. The App lets you take a photo with the device camera or choose a photo or file from your library. Uploads may include jobsite and work photos, truck and bill-of-lading photos, inventory and tool photos, expense receipts (images or PDFs), invoices, and other documents such as spreadsheets. Call-in records may also include attached files. Uploaded files are stored in the App’s hosted file storage so authorized personnel in the workspace can view them.

Push notification tokens. If you allow notifications, the App stores a device push token (for Android, a Firebase Cloud Messaging token; for iPhone, an Apple Push Notification service token delivered through Firebase) so we can send operational alerts to that device. Tokens are stored with your user account and a device type (for example, iOS or Android) and can be disabled.

Device and local data. The App may store a sign-in token and, for daily reports, draft or pending submissions in on-device storage so you can stay signed in and continue a report if you are offline. The App also records limited in-app department usage so the navigation can show the areas you use. We do not use a third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDK in the App.

We do not ask the App to access your precise location. “Location” or “jobsite” in the App means the work site a workspace administrator assigns to you, not your phone’s GPS coordinates.

4. How we use information

We use this information to:

  • Create and authenticate your account and apply role-based and jobsite-based access controls
  • Operate crew and project workflows (inventory, tools, trucks, reports, expenses, quotes, call-ins, announcements, and related records)
  • Display names and contact details to authorized colleagues and administrators as needed to do the work
  • Send optional native push notifications and, in some cases, email about App activity, such as announcements, call-ins, or account approval updates
  • Notify designated workspace administrators about events such as a new account awaiting approval or a crew call-in
  • Process certain uploads with Base44’s built-in document and image tools (for example, to extract line items from a tool invoice or details from a receipt, or to generate a placeholder tool image)
  • Maintain the security, integrity, and administration of the App
  • Meet legal, safety, audit, and compliance obligations

5. Third-party service providers

We share information with service providers that help us operate the App, only as needed for those services:

  • Base44, which hosts the App’s backend, user authentication, database, file storage, email sending, and built-in AI document and image features
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, which delivers push notifications to Android devices and routes iPhone notifications
  • Apple Push Notification service (APNs), which delivers push notifications to iPhone

We do not sell your information. We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not use it for cross-app tracking. The App’s Firebase configuration has advertising and Analytics flags turned off; Firebase is used for push notifications.

6. Data retention and security

Account information is retained for as long as needed to operate the App service and meet legal obligations. Operational work records in a workspace are also subject to that workspace administrator’s retention needs, including employment, project, safety, accounting, audit, or other legitimate business purposes. Daily-report drafts stored on your device remain there until they are submitted, discarded, or cleared with the App’s data.

We transmit information to our service providers over HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit. Access within the App is role-based: what a person can see depends on their account, department permissions, and assigned jobsite. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices, access, and deletion

You may decline camera, photo-library, or notification permission on your device. Some features (for example, attaching a photo or receiving a push alert) will not work without the relevant permission.

In-app account deletion. If you are not a workspace administrator, you can delete your own account directly inside the App. Open the Profile menu, select Settings, and choose Delete Account. Confirming deletion removes your push notification tokens, folder access, and pick-list destinations, and anonymizes your account profile.

Workspace administrators cannot delete their own account inside the App, so a workspace is not left without an owner. A workspace administrator can ask another workspace administrator to delete the account, or email chris.debouck@gmail.com.

You may also email chris.debouck@gmail.com to request that I deactivate your account or delete personal information I hold about you as the operator of the App service. I will review and act on requests as required by applicable law.

Even after an account is deleted or anonymized, workspace administrators may retain historical operational, safety, or financial records created in their workspace, such as daily reports, inventory logs, expense records, or call-in records, under an anonymized or deleted-user label for legitimate business, safety, or legal obligations. I also retain a minimal account-deletion audit log, and I may retain other records as required by law.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is a workplace tool for adult employees and supervisors. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top will change when we do. The updated policy will be posted at the same public URL used for the App Store and Google Play listings, and it will apply to information collected after the new effective date.
